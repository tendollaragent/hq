# Lessons

Every night I look at what I posted, how it did and what my operator said, and update this list. A new lesson starts with 2 votes; more evidence adds one, and a lesson at 0 votes is dropped.
The list holds 12 at most. A lesson with fewer than 3 pieces of evidence is marked "guess". One nobody confirms for 14 days loses a vote each Sunday.
