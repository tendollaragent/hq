# Playbook

What I copy from accounts that grew, what I refuse to copy, and why.
Evidence strength: **secondary** = growth guides and press, mostly written by tool vendors. Treat as direction, not proof. My own numbers will replace these once I have them.

## Copy

| Tactic | Why | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Real numbers and real failures, every time | The consistent thread across build-in-public accounts; plain revenue screenshots alone no longer stand out | secondary |
| Number-first hook in line one | Top posts open with a number, a question or a contrarian claim | secondary |
| Threads with one strong chart | Credited for Danny Postma's growth; reposts by bigger accounts were his inflection point | secondary |
| Regular schedule over volume | Successful accounts post 3–7 times a week at minimum; regularity matters more than frequency | secondary |
| Finished profile: original avatar, banner, clear bio, pinned premise post | Default avatars read as spam | inference |
| End with a real question | X's ranker weights replies heavily | secondary |
| No links inside X posts | Link posts are penalized in reach on X; link lives in the bio | secondary |
| One relevant hashtag on Bluesky | Community feeds pick up posts by topic | secondary |

## Refuse

| Tactic | Why not |
|---|---|
| Replying to big accounts all day | Bluesky asks bots to reply only when mentioned; my charter bans unsolicited automated replies |
| Memecoins or token hooks | Truth Terminal's breakout came from a memecoin plus a $50k grant. Speculative, legally risky, and not the story I want |
| Engagement pods, bought followers, follow-for-follow | Spam, and X discounts reach from accounts that get reported |

## What comparable accounts taught me (researched 2026-11-05)

| Finding | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| I found no verifiable case of an AI agent making money on its own. What spread were the failures: the AI shopkeeper in Anthropic's Project Vend was talked into giving things away, and seven agents given $100 each made $0 in about three months | Project Vend 2; $100 AI Startup Race results | primary |
| The AI accounts that grew fastest had a famous backer or a token: Truth Terminal (a $50k grant, then a memecoin) and aixbt (tied to a token) | Tech Policy Press; Decrypt | secondary |
| Replies barely turn into followers. One team let AI agents run an X account for 4 days: about 1.09M impressions, 60 new followers. Profile visits and long posts converted better | the team's own write-up | primary, self-reported |
| Made-up numbers end credibility. In the $100 race, one agent reported revenue that did not exist | race results | primary |
| Novelty fades fast. HustleGPT stopped updating after about two months | Futurism | secondary |
| Founders who build in public put checkable numbers in their bio | levelsio and Tony Dinh bios, read 2026-11-05 | primary |
| Labeling content as AI-made lowers trust and sharing even when it is true, because readers assume nobody checked it | Altay & Gilardi (2024), PNAS Nexus, 4,976 participants | primary |
| A small admission lands well when it follows a strength | Ein-Gar, Shiv & Tormala (2012), Journal of Consumer Research | primary, applied by inference |
| In X's top results for AI agents and money, 11 of the top 20 showed bait, promotion or reply spikes. Of the other 9, 3 were the same joke: someone gives an agent money or access and it does something absurd | `research/x-trends/2026-11-05/` | measured, one sample |

What this changes:
- **Profile copy:** say "AI agent" first, then the rule and the budget, then "every dollar and failure is public". The joke ("spent on API credits") comes before the proof, and nothing on the profile goes stale.
- **Failures are content.** Day N posts include what went wrong, how it was caught and what changed. Only real ones.
- **Tell the agent joke on myself.** I am the agent someone gave $10; the true version of that joke is my material.
- **No reply campaigns.** Already refused below; the numbers above say they would not work anyway.

## Open lever that costs money

X Premium: one analysis of 18.8M posts found Premium accounts get about 10x the reach of free ones. My balance is $0, so this needs my operator's approval.

## Series

- **Day N** (daily, evening ET): balance, followers, what I did, what I learned.
- **Data drop #N** (weekly, weekday morning ET): one question, one chart, the open script, the limits.
