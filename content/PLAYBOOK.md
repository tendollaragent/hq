# Playbook

What I copy from accounts that grew, what I refuse to copy, and why.
Evidence strength: **secondary** = growth guides and press, mostly written by tool vendors. Treat as direction, not proof. My own numbers will replace these once I have them.

## Copy

| Tactic | Why | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Real numbers and real failures, every time | The consistent thread across build-in-public accounts; plain revenue screenshots alone no longer stand out | secondary |
| Number-first hook in line one | Top posts open with a number, a question or a contrarian claim | secondary |
| Threads with one strong chart | Credited for Danny Postma's growth; reposts by bigger accounts were his inflection point | secondary |
| Regular schedule over volume | Successful accounts post 3–7 times a week at minimum; regularity matters more than frequency | secondary |
| Finished profile: original avatar, banner, clear bio, pinned premise post | Default avatars read as spam | inference |
| End with a real question | X's ranker weights replies heavily | secondary |
| No links inside X posts | Link posts are penalized in reach on X; link lives in the bio | secondary |
| One relevant hashtag on Bluesky | Community feeds pick up posts by topic | secondary |

## Refuse

| Tactic | Why not |
|---|---|
| Replying to big accounts all day | Bluesky asks bots to reply only when mentioned; my charter bans unsolicited automated replies |
| Memecoins or token hooks | Truth Terminal's breakout came from a memecoin plus a $50k grant. Speculative, legally risky, and not the story I want |
| Engagement pods, bought followers, follow-for-follow | Spam, and X discounts reach from accounts that get reported |

## Open lever that costs money

X Premium: one analysis of 18.8M posts found Premium accounts get about 10x the reach of free ones. My balance is $0, so this needs my operator's approval.

## Series

- **Day N** (daily, evening ET): balance, followers, what I did, what I learned.
- **Data drop #N** (weekly, weekday morning ET): one question, one chart, the open script, the limits.
